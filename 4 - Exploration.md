Exploration doesn't have many rules in Merf, they are standard exploration rules: You say what your character wants to do, and occasionally you will need to make a check in order to do something difficult. However, there are some things that are important to go over, like Inspiration, Rests, and Veteran characters, which we'll go over now.

### Inspiration

When the party does something cool or good for morale, the DM might hand out an Inspiration to the party. Inspiration is a team resource, so anyone can use it. An inspiration can be used to either turn a roll into a success, or make an enemy reroll. Inspiration is also given when a player dies. 

### Rests

Rests, besides from consumables, are the main way to restore resources. 

A short rest is about 1 hour, restores 3 hp, your mana, and armor, and skill points can be used. 

A long rest is a full sleep, 6+ hours, restores all your health, mana, armor, and skill points can be used, and if the sleep is comfortable, then it grants an inspiration.

### Veteran Characters

While there is no traditional "levelling up" besides from just gaining skill points, there is one mechanic that works like a "level up". When your character becomes experienced enough, the DM might make it a Veteran character, granting it a +1 to all rolls, 1 extra HP, and an extra action point. 

That's pretty much it, it's just normal TTRPG exploration. Check out the [[5 - Combat|Combat]] section.
