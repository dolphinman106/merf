Tech mods are the other big customization feature in Merf. While you can't make up tech mods on the spot like Aether, you'll find they aren't lacking in customization. You only get tech mods if you play a Cyborg or a Synthman, and the amount of tech slots you get also depends on which one you pick. 

As a Cyborg, you get 5 slots - one for each limb, and one for your torso. As a synthman, you get an additional slot for your head. You can find tech mods around the world or from a Modder - "trained professionals" that can install them for you, or you can try and install them yourself.
